Employee Performance & Prediction Pipeline

An end-to-end data mining platform that predicts promotion readiness, flight risk, and compensation equity with full transparency through data ingestion, preprocessing, SMOTE imbalance balancing, ensemble classification, and explainable AI.

Create Account Sign In View Data Mining Pipeline →
Stage 1 & 2

Preprocessing & Imputation

Handles real-world null values via Median/Mode imputation, caps continuous outliers via IQR Winsorization, and normalizes with StandardScaler.

Stage 3 & 4

SMOTE Imbalance Balancing

Mitigates extreme ~17% positive class imbalance using Synthetic Minority Over-sampling along k-nearest neighbors to eliminate majority bias.

Stage 5 & 6

Ensemble Classification

Trains and evaluates Logistic Regression, Random Forest (Bagging), and Gradient Boosting (Sequential) with 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation.

Stage 7 & 8

Bias-Variance & XAI

Analyzes generalization gaps, convergence learning curves, and delivers local SHAP-style positive catalysts and risk factor explanations.
